# Privacy Policy

**App:** Kanji Desu Ne («Кандзи дэс нэ»), Android, `ru.mobui.kanji`  
**Developer:** Sergey Chabotarev  
**Privacy contact:** [sachebotarev@gmail.com](mailto:sachebotarev@gmail.com)  
**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

## 1. Scope

This policy explains how Kanji Desu Ne handles data while you study Japanese characters and vocabulary. No registration or app account is required.

## 2. Data on your device

The app stores your personal vocabulary (words, expressions, sentences, readings, translations in selected languages and tags), recipes (saved training configurations), training history, task times and results, scores, hint usage and app settings in internal device storage. This enables vocabulary management, practice, progress tracking and saved preferences.

The app does not send your vocabulary, recipes or training history to a developer server. The developer has no server access to these records. System backups are described in section 4.

Handwriting strokes are processed on your device to check shape, stroke order and character recognition. Handwriting is not sent to the developer or Google for cloud recognition.

## 3. Google ML Kit

The app uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition for legibility checks. The Japanese recognition model is downloaded from Google over the internet. Recognition then runs on your device.

ML Kit also sends technical information to Google for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics. Google's documentation lists device and operating system information, app package and version, installation identifiers and, depending on the SDK component type, device identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration and version, input and output sizes, operational and model download events, error codes and configured languages. Input and output sizes do not mean the content of handwriting is uploaded.

Google states that this diagnostic data is transmitted over HTTPS and is not transferred by ML Kit to third parties. See [ML Kit data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

The app has no advertising or separately integrated advertising tracking services. ML Kit diagnostics are described above.

## 4. Android backups

Standard Android backup is enabled. Depending on the operating system, device manufacturer, backup settings and available limits, local app data may be included in a system cloud backup or transferred to another device. Cloud copies may be stored by Google or your device's backup provider.

The developer cannot access these copies. Manage backup and restore through Android and your backup provider's settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its device data does not guarantee deletion of existing cloud backups; restoring a backup may restore your records.

## 5. Access and security

The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone. Internet access supports Google ML Kit components, including model downloads and technical diagnostics.

The local database and preferences use internal app storage protected by Android access controls. Protection also depends on device security, screen lock and backup settings. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

The developer does not sell user data or provide personal vocabulary, recipes or training history to advertisers.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete the relevant entries or clear app data. You can clear training history in the app settings. To remove all local data, use Android Settings → Apps → Kanji Desu Ne → Storage → Clear data; labels may vary. Uninstalling also removes local app data through standard Android mechanisms.

Backup retention and deletion depend on your backup provider's settings and policies. Google determines retention of the technical information it receives. The developer does not set those periods and has no direct access to delete that information. See [Google's privacy controls](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en#infochoices).

There are no app accounts and no separate account deletion process.

## 7. Contacting the developer

If you email [sachebotarev@gmail.com](mailto:sachebotarev@gmail.com), the developer receives your sender address, message and any attachments. This information is used to respond and address your request. Please send only information needed for the request.

Correspondence is kept in Gmail while the request and necessary follow-up communication are handled. Contact the same address to request deletion of correspondence or ask about data handling. Google's policies also apply to Gmail's processing of email.

## 8. Children

The app does not require a child's age or other personal details. The ML Kit technical diagnostics described above apply to app use. If you believe a child has sent personal data to the developer, contact the developer to discuss deletion.

## 9. Policy changes

This policy may change when app features or data handling change. The current version will be published on this page with its update date.
