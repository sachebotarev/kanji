Kanji learning catalog / Учебный каталог кандзи

kanji.csv includes data derived from KANJIDIC2, copyright James William
Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).
KANJIDIC2 database version: 2026-293, created 2026-10-20.
https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Part of the initial Russian wording was adapted from the Russian Wikipedia
list of Joyo kanji, by its contributors. It was shortened and corrected.
https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Список_дзёё-кандзи
https://ru.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Список_дзёё-кандзи&action=history

The derived kanji.csv dataset is available under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: selection of educational/Joyo/Jinmeiyo characters, addition of
the 2026 Jinmeiyo character 勒, Russian editing, radical mapping, reading
variant fallback for four entries, and CSV conversion.

YARXI (Vadim Smolensky; web implementation by Seva Alekseyev) and WARODAI
were consulted for verification. Their complete dictionary articles and
databases are not included in this package.
https://yarxi.ru/online/
https://www.warodai.ru/about/

See project documentation: doc/implementation/kanji-catalog.md.

Before exposing these data in application UI, provide the source and
licence acknowledgement required by EDRDG in an accessible Sources/About
screen and establish an update procedure.


Basic Kanji Book I-II import vocabulary

basic-kanji-book.csv: educational import dataset prepared from the community
Anki deck Basic Kanji Books vol 1 2, publisher revision examples, and
university lesson indexes. Russian wording was edited for recall practice;
matching Tobira entries retain their existing translations. Source review,
versions, changes and unresolved issues: doc/imports/basic-kanji-book/README.md.
https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/3174372445
https://publishing.bonjinsha.co.jp/support/bkb
https://contsrv.icer.kyushu-u.ac.jp/web/QKANJI/index_vol1.html
https://contsrv.icer.kyushu-u.ac.jp/web/QKANJI/index_vol2.html

JMdict (EDRDG) was consulted for dictionary checks.
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Reading segmentation includes adapted JmdictFurigana data, release
2.3.1+2026-09-25, plus editorial additions.
https://github.com/Doublevil/JmdictFurigana
